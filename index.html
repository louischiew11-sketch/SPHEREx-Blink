Every source, visit date and flag on this page is simulated so the interface can be tried offline. To show real SPHEREx data, replace build() with a loader that fills S.imgs[visit][array] with co-added N×N cutouts and edit the EP list of visits; nothing else needs to change. Preset coordinates are approximate and the demo centres each object at the middle visit.
